# Lumo — Communication Access in Maternal Healthcare

**Project type:** Collaborative human-centered design concept (2022)  
**Team:** Maya Joseph, Brygitte De La Cruz, Tiffany Chen, Katherine Ju, Yicheng (Rico) Li  
**Status:** Proposed concept and prototype, not a deployed medical product

## Design challenge
How might we help non-English-speaking mothers feel heard, informed, and more autonomous during prenatal and postpartum medical visits?

## Discovery
The team conducted exploratory interviews with mothers and consulted an OB-GYN. These conversations raised concerns about interpreter wait times, reliance on third parties, difficulty retaining information, and the loss of emotional nuance in clinical conversations.

## Design process
1. Interviews and stakeholder research
2. Problem definition and alternative solution concepts
3. Early prototypes and feedback
4. Iteration toward a translation-focused tablet experience
5. Storyboarding, presentation, and process documentation

## Proposed experience
Lumo combined earpieces with a tablet interface to facilitate live translated conversation, a visible transcript, and bookmarks for information patients wanted to revisit. The team considered consent for saving or discarding transcriptions.

## Design limitations
This classroom concept was not clinically validated. Medical translation accuracy, patient safety, consent, privacy, and integration with professional interpretation services would require further testing before implementation.
